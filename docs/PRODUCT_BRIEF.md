# Product Brief — Arya Wiguna Portfolio

## Product
Personal portfolio website for Arya Wiguna.

## Primary audience
- Recruiters and hiring managers.
- Engineering leads.
- Potential collaborators and clients.

## Primary user questions
1. Who is Arya?
2. What kind of software does Arya build?
3. What projects has Arya actually contributed to?
4. What technologies has Arya used in practice?
5. How can someone contact Arya or review the work?

## Positioning
A software engineer/developer who builds practical web and mobile applications. Keep the positioning accurate to verified experience; do not imply seniority or expertise without evidence.

## Design direction
Modern Editorial + Subtle 3D:
- Warm off-white canvas.
- Deep navy typography.
- Restrained blue accents.
- Strong typographic hierarchy.
- Generous whitespace.
- Real project screenshots.
- One optional, lightweight 3D hero accent.

## Brand qualities
Thoughtful, precise, calm, technically credible, approachable, independent.

## Non-goals
- Not a SaaS landing page.
- Not a generic AI-generated portfolio.
- Not a cyberpunk/galaxy experience.
- Not a 3D game or interactive résumé.
- Not a wall of technology logos or certificate badges.

## Success criteria
- Visitors understand profile and strongest work quickly.
- Project details demonstrate problem-solving and individual contribution.
- Contact and CV are easy to access.
- Mobile experience is complete.
- Fast, accessible, search-friendly pages.
- All claims and links are verifiable.
